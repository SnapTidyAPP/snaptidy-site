What SnapTidy does

  • Clears out your camera roll a few photos at a time: swipe right to keep, left to mark, down to add a photo to an album.
  • Swiping never deletes anything. Photos are removed only after you review the set and confirm, and they stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.
  • The Tidy Assistant groups accidental shots, blurry photos, extra burst frames and old screenshots, and shows why each was picked.
  • Everything runs on the device. No account, no ads, no third-party tracking.

The free version allows 40 decisions a day. Pro removes the limit.
