About

SnapTidy is a photo cleaner for iPhone and iPad that works a few photos at a time. It gives you a set of 20, 40 or 60 photos: swipe right to keep, left to mark, down to add a photo to an album. Swiping never deletes anything. Photos are removed only after you review the set and confirm, and they stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Version 1.1 added the Tidy Assistant, which groups accidental shots, blurry photos, extra burst frames and old screenshots and shows why each was picked. It is cautious with photos of people and pets. Pre-trip Cleanup downloads a set of photos before a flight so you can sort them offline on board, and albums can be sorted by trip or by theme and saved to the Photos app. Quick Cleanup collects screenshots, large videos and similar photos, Live Photos included.

Photos and analysis stay on the device. There is no account, no ads and no third-party tracking.
